Our word on it

Every cut, verified.

All chicken and beef served at Two Cuts is sourced exclusively from certified halal abattoirs, hand-slaughtered per Islamic law, and kept separate from prep to plate. Our kitchen has never handled pork or alcohol-based marinades — not as policy, but as the whole reason this place exists. Ask any server for our supplier's certification; we keep it posted at the counter, not just on the wall.
